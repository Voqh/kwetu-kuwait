1. What Kwetu is

Kwetu Kuwait is a free community listings board that helps people find and post rooms, partitions, and bedspaces in Kuwait, organized by area. Kwetu does not own, manage, inspect, or verify any accommodation listed on the platform, is not a party to any rental agreement, and does not process any payments between users.

3. No accounts, no verified identities

Kwetu does not require sign-up or login. Anyone can post or browse. We do not verify the identity of accommodation providers, the accuracy of listings, or the legitimacy of accommodation offered. You are responsible for exercising your own judgment — meet in safe public places when possible, verify details independently, and never send money to someone you haven't met or verified through your own diligence.

4. Posting a listing

In these terms, an accommodation provider is the person offering accommodation through a listing. By posting, you confirm that:

  • You have the right to advertise the accommodation described.
  • The information you provide (area, block, description, rent, contact number) is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
  • You will not post discriminatory, fraudulent, misleading, or illegal content.
  • You consent to your WhatsApp number being displayed publicly on the listing so interested people can contact you directly.

Before a listing is published or an edit is saved, you must actively tick the consent checkbox on the review step. It confirms that you have the right to advertise the accommodation, that you consent to your WhatsApp number being shown publicly, and that you agree to these Terms and our Privacy Policy. Publishing is blocked until you do so.

We may remove any listing, without notice, that we believe violates these terms, is fraudulent, or has been reported by multiple users (see our reporting system).

5. Reporting and moderation

Users can report listings they believe are fraudulent, inappropriate, or inaccurate. Listings that accumulate enough reports are automatically hidden pending review. We do this to keep the platform trustworthy, not as a guarantee that every visible listing has been verified.

6. Listing expiry

Listings automatically stop appearing 30 days after posting. At that same 30-day mark, your WhatsApp number is permanently deleted from our database. The rest of the listing is deleted permanently 7 days after that. This is by design, to keep the board current — we don't offer a way to extend a listing beyond this without reposting.
